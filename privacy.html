Privacy policy

ReelSync GTA is a personal tool used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission it uses the owner's own Google account to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and to change those videos' visibility.

What it stores

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. Nothing is stored on any server, and no data is sold, shared or sent to anyone else.

Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
